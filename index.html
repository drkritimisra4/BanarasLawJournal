About the Journal

Founded in 1965 under the leadership of Justice N. H. Bhagwati, then Vice-Chancellor of Banaras Hindu University, and Professor Anand Jee, then Dean of the Law School, the Banaras Law Journal was the first of its kind published by an Indian university. It remains one of the country's earliest and most established platforms for peer-reviewed legal scholarship — publishing articles, notes, and book reviews from scholars, judges, practitioners, and students, biannually, under the Faculty of Law.
